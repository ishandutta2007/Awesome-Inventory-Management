# Awesome-Inventory-Management

## Top Inventory Management Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Stock Control, Multi-Warehouse Tracking, Order Fulfillment, Manufacturing Inventory, Barcode/Lot Tracking & Multichannel Sync*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Inventory Management**. These tools help businesses track stock levels, manage warehouses, handle purchase and sales orders, support manufacturing/BOM workflows, and keep inventory synchronized across sales channels.

**Examples** include Cin7, Katana, Zoho Inventory, DEAR Systems (Cin7 Core), Unleashed, Sortly, Fishbowl, NetSuite Inventory Management, Finale Inventory, and Skubana (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Inventory management is one of the strongest categories for open-source software. Mature, production-ready systems such as InvenTree, ERPNext, and Odoo provide full stock control, multi-warehouse support, and manufacturing capabilities. This section is heavily expanded with every major active project.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[InvenTree](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree)**  
  Leading open-source inventory management system focused on parts, components, and stock control. Features a powerful REST API, plugin system, barcode support, and excellent low-level inventory precision.

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Fully open-source ERP with robust inventory, warehouse, purchasing, sales, and manufacturing modules. Handles multi-warehouse stock, serial/lot tracking, and complex operational workflows.

- **[Odoo Community (Inventory / Stock)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Modular open-source ERP whose Inventory app provides warehouse management, stock moves, barcodes, lot/serial tracking, and integration with sales, purchasing, and manufacturing.

- **[MyCompany (lsFusion)](https://github.com/lsfusion-solutions/mycompany)**  
  Free, self-hosted open-source ERP/CRM for small businesses covering inventory, purchasing, sales, manufacturing, and POS in a single system.

- **[Inventory Tools for ERPNext](https://github.com/agritheory/inventory_tools)**  
  Extensions and enhancements that streamline inventory management and manufacturing workflows on top of ERPNext.

- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**  
  Open-source asset management system often used for IT and fixed-asset inventory (complementary to pure stock/SKU inventory systems).

- **[Other CMMS / stock systems](https://github.com/search?q=open+source+inventory+management)**  
  Additional community projects offering lighter-weight stock tracking, barcode scanning, and warehouse features.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Barcode & labeling**: Tools and libraries for generating and scanning barcodes/QR codes integrated with inventory systems.
- **Mobile stock apps**: Companion mobile applications (including InvenTree’s official app) for receiving, picking, and stocktaking.
- **Manufacturing / BOM extensions**: Open modules for bills of materials, work orders, and component consumption.
- **Multichannel connectors**: Community integrations that sync stock with ecommerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.).
- **Reporting & analytics**: Dashboards built with Metabase, Superset, or native ERP reporting for stock valuation and movement analysis.
- Various specialized forks and vertical adaptations of the major open-source inventory systems.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
For pure inventory and parts management, start with **InvenTree**.  
For broader operational needs (accounting, CRM, manufacturing), deploy **ERPNext** or **Odoo Community**.  
Add barcode hardware, mobile clients, and ecommerce connectors as required.  
This combination can fully replace many mid-market commercial inventory platforms while giving complete data ownership and customization freedom.
